How to use this portal

  1. Log in with your matric number and password.
  2. View your academic dashboard and current session updates.
  3. Click on the menu items to access courses, registration, results, and profile details.
  4. Use the registration form to enter your academic information.
  5. Check your course list and total credits for the semester.
  6. If you forget your password, use the reset option on the login page.
