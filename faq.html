How to Use the Portal

Back to Login

Login

  1. Open the Dominion University login page.
  2. Enter your matric number in the first field.
  3. Enter your password in the password field.
  4. Click the Log in button to access your portal.

Forgot Password

  1. Click the Forgot Password? Click here! link.
  2. Enter the email connected to your student account.
  3. Receive your 6-digit verification code.
  4. Enter the code and create a new password.

After Login

  1. View your dashboard and academic information.
  2. Check your student registration details and course records.
  3. Use the menu to navigate to courses, results, profile, and payments.
  4. Use the help button on the dashboard if you need guidance.
