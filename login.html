Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in?

Enter your matric number and password in the form, then click the Log in button.

What if I forgot my password?

Click “Forgot Password? Click here!” and enter your registered email to receive a 6-digit code.

Where do I get my details?

Your matric number and password are provided by the student admin or registration office.

What can I do after logging in?

You can view your dashboard, check registration details, review courses, and access your student information.
